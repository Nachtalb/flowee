Third-party software in the Flowee container image
==================================================

musl (/lib/ld-musl-*.so.1)
  Licence: MIT. Text: musl-COPYRIGHT.txt
  Source:  https://musl.libc.org/ (as packaged by Alpine Linux: https://pkgs.alpinelinux.org/package/v3.24/main/x86_64/musl)

  Texts:   GPL-3.0-or-later.txt, GCC-exception-3.1.txt

  Source:  https://pkgs.alpinelinux.org/package/v3.24/main/x86_64/ca-certificates
